## The pricing difference that actually matters

The two tools price in different ways, and that changes which one is cheaper for you.

**Kahunas prices by client count.** Essentials covers up to 25 clients, Growth up to 50 and Ultimate is unlimited. Paying yearly brings Essentials down to the equivalent of $26.25 a month and Growth to $51.75 a month. Every plan already includes unlimited workout and nutrition plans and the full food database, so you're mainly paying for more clients and extra features like automations.

**myPTHub prices by features and add-ons.** The jump from Starter (3 clients) to Premium (unlimited clients) is big, so most working PTs will be looking at Premium or above. From there, you add what you need: a branded app, extra trainers, Check-Ins AI or Zapier. Add-on prices are shown in US dollars, and you'll need to check the subscription prices on myPTHub's pricing page, as they vary with offers and billing period.

A quick way to compare: count your current clients, estimate where you'll be in a year, and list the extras you'd actually use. Then price both options against that list, not against the headline plan.

## Branded apps: read the small print

Both tools can put your own brand in front of clients, but the costs work differently.

With Kahunas, the own branded app with your own App Store listings comes with the Ultimate plan. You'll also need an Apple developer account, which Kahunas says costs $99 USD a year, and it says 99% of apps go live within 30 days.

With myPTHub, there are two levels. A custom branded app is a one-time $95 add-on. A white label app with your own store listing is $145 a month as an add-on, or included in the Ultimate plan.

If a branded app is high on your list, work out the yearly cost of each route, including any developer account fees.
