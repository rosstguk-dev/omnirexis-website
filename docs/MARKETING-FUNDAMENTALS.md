## 5. Brand voice

Plain, human, specific, dry, a bit funny. Write like Ross talking to another operator. UK English: organise, optimise, programme, enquiry, £, "per month" or "/month".

**Use:** enquiries, follow-up, admin, the floor, session pack, check-in, clients, bookings, set up, connect, save time, start free, book a call, fall through the cracks, part of the workforce.

**Avoid:** revolutionary, game-changing, unleash, cutting-edge, next-gen, supercharge, disrupt, synergy, leverage, seamless, AI-powered everything, robot/sci-fi clichés ("the future is here", "your AI army"), "practical AI for operators" filler, jargon (LLM, agentic, workflow orchestration). No em or en dashes. Emoji: one at most, optional.

**Caption formula:** What (what we do) · Who (who it is for) · Why (why it matters) · one CTA. If a draft fails the three questions, do not post it.
